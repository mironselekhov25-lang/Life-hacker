## 1. 🧠 The 2-Minute Rule
If a task takes less than two minutes, do it immediately. Small actions like replying to a message or putting something away reduce mental clutter and often lead to bigger productivity streaks once momentum kicks in.


---


## 2. ⏰ Smart Alarms = Smart Brain
Use phone alarms with labels like *"Drink water"* or *"Start homework."* This turns your phone into an external memory system, reducing stress and preventing forgotten tasks.


---


## 3. 🧹 One-Touch Cleaning Rule
Only touch an item once. Instead of moving clutter around, put it directly where it belongs. This simple rule drastically cuts down cleaning time and mess buildup.


---


## 4. 📸 Screenshot With Purpose
Create a folder called **Read Later** on your phone. Move useful screenshots there weekly. This prevents screenshots from becoming digital clutter and makes information easier to revisit.


---


## 5. 🎮 Gamify Boring Tasks
Turn chores into games. Set a 10-minute timer and see how much you can finish. Racing the clock makes dull tasks more engaging and rewarding.


---


## 6. 🍎 Eat What You See
Keep healthy snacks at eye level and hide junk food in hard-to-reach places. Convenience often beats willpower when it comes to food choices.


---


## 7. ✍️ Brain Dump for Peace of Mind
Write down thoughts, worries, or to-do items when your mind feels overloaded. This signals your brain that it’s handled, reducing anxiety—especially before sleep.


---


## 8. 🎧 Focus With Background Noise
Use white noise, rain sounds, or café ambience to reduce distractions. These sounds help your brain stay focused, especially in noisy environments.


---


## 9. 🌙 Prep Tomorrow Tonight
Lay out clothes, pack bags, or write tomorrow’s top three tasks before bed. This minimizes morning stress and decision fatigue.


---


## 10. 🧑‍🏫 Learn by Teaching
Explain what you learn out loud—even to yourself. Teaching strengthens understanding and memory, making knowledge stick longer.
